How to Add Your Signature to Gmail

  1. Generate your signature using the tools above.
  2. Click the "Copy Rich Signature" button.
  3. Open Gmail and click the Settings icon (top right).
  4. Select "See all settings".
  5. Scroll down to the "Signature" section in the "General" tab.
  6. Click "+ Create new" or select an existing signature.
  7. Paste (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V) your signature into the text area.
  8. Scroll to the bottom and click "Save Changes".
